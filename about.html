Our Story

Who We Are

Blueray Transport LTD. was founded in 2021 during the challenges of the pandemic. The company was built on a foundation of hard work, trucking experience, strong family values, and a commitment to providing dependable freight transportation.

As a family-owned and family-operated business based in Abbotsford, British Columbia, we understand the importance of trust, communication, and reliability. We work hard to build lasting relationships with customers by providing professional service from pickup through delivery.

Our goal is not simply to move freight. We aim to become a transportation partner that businesses can depend on for consistent service, honest communication, and responsible handling of every shipment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide safe, reliable, and efficient transportation services that businesses can depend on throughout Western Canada.
